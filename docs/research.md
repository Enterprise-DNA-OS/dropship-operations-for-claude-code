Checked 17 November 2026. New family: dropship-supplier-operations. The scope is retailer-to-supplier acknowledgement, ship promises and partial dispatch review, distinct from the existing warehouse and omnichannel fulfilment builds.

The [vendor pricing page](https://duoplane.com/pricing/) lists Premium at $1,199 monthly, or $14,388 for twelve monthly payments before overage and implementation. The page displays dollar symbols without naming the billing currency. No NZ or AU customer invoice was verified. Standard is $299 monthly and Professional $599. All plans list unlimited vendors, users and ecommerce channels. Do not describe this as per-seat pricing.

Local customer notes and CRM snapshots returned no confirmed subscription; no live CRM read was performed. Search results contained comparison pages but supplied no search-volume evidence. Buyer fit is therefore weakly evidenced and scored conservatively.

It scored 2/4/2/2/3/3 = 16 because this run established a clearer Duoplane export path. Orderful was excluded because its trading-partner connectivity and network are core to the product.
